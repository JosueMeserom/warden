      What gives X-ray away: hidden diamonds (no face exposed to air, water or lava) per 1000 stone blocks mined. Mining blind gives ~1-4. Those in caves don't count, and the world's lifetime total is only a rough guide (it includes caves).

      Areas where someone has placed lots of blocks (since the plugin was installed).
